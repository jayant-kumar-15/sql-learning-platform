Get in Touch

If you have a question, suggestion, technical issue, content correction, or general feedback about SQL Learning Platform, we'd like to hear from you.

Your feedback helps us improve the learning experience and identify areas where the platform can be made more useful.

How You Can Contact Us

  • Platform Feedback Use the feedback functionality available on the SQL Learning Platform to submit suggestions, comments, or general feedback.
  • Technical Issues If you encounter a broken feature, unexpected behavior, incorrect result, or other technical problem, please provide as much relevant detail as possible through the available feedback functionality.
  • Content Corrections If you believe a tutorial, SQL example, challenge, explanation, or other educational material contains an error, please let us know so it can be reviewed.

What to Include in Your Message

When reporting an issue or suggesting an improvement, including the following information can make it easier to understand the request:

  • The page or feature involved
  • A short description of the issue
  • The SQL query involved, when relevant
  • The expected result and actual result
  • Any relevant error message
  • Steps that reproduce the issue, if applicable

Protect Your Information

Please do not include sensitive information such as passwords, authentication credentials, real patient information, financial account information, confidential business information, or other private data in feedback or support messages.

Please use sample or non-sensitive information when describing a technical issue whenever possible.

Response Times

We aim to review feedback and issues as reasonably possible. However, a response is not guaranteed for every submission, and response times may vary.

Platform Support

The contact and feedback functionality is intended primarily for questions and issues related to SQL Learning Platform, its educational content, practice environments, and available features.

Privacy

Information submitted through available contact or feedback functionality may be processed for purposes such as responding to requests, improving the platform, identifying technical problems, and maintaining platform quality.

For additional information about how information is handled, please review our Privacy Policy .

Thank You

Thank you for using SQL Learning Platform and helping us make the platform better for SQL learners.
